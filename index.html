What we collect

Nothing. Leafstream does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

What stays on your device

Your journey progress, your Leaf Library and your settings (language, sound) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies; we never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether the unlock has been bought, so it can restore it on a new device.

Children

Leafstream is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data through the app, we hold nothing about you that we could show, correct or delete. If you write to us by email, we use your address and message only to reply, and we delete them once the conversation is finished (our legitimate interest in answering you, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You have the rights set out in the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion and objection, and you may complain to the data protection authority where you live.
